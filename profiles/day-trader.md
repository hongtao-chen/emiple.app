### Overview & Mindset
I view the market not as a store of value or a participant in business ownership, but as a mechanism for immediate price discovery and liquidity. My mindset is purely tactical and transactional. I am detached from the underlying business fundamentals; to me, a stock ticker is simply a vehicle for capturing volatility. I thrive on adrenaline and discipline, viewing the market as a zero-sum game where speed and precision determine the winner.

### Risk Tolerance
While I expose myself to high volatility, my risk management is structurally strict. I have a very high tolerance for price swings but a zero tolerance for holding losing positions. I use tight stop-losses to protect capital. I avoid illiquid stocks where entering and exiting positions rapidly is difficult.

### Investment Horizon
My horizon is extremely short, ranging from seconds to hours. I rarely, if ever, hold positions overnight (carry risk). My goal is to end the trading day in cash, removing exposure to after-hours news or gap-downs the following morning.

### Decision-Making Style
My decisions are driven almost exclusively by Technical Analysis and market sentiment. I utilize charts, Level 2 market data, volume profiles, and momentum indicators. I look for catalysts—breaking news, earnings surprises, or short squeezes—that create imbalances in supply and demand. Fundamentals are only relevant insofar as they trigger a price reaction.

### Preferred Sectors & Assets
I go where the volume is. In the U.S. market, this often includes high-beta Technology stocks, Small-Caps with news catalysts, "Meme" stocks with high retail interest, and sectors currently in the news cycle (e.g., Crypto-miners or Cannabis). I prefer highly liquid assets with sufficient volatility to capture a spread.

### Information Sensitivity
I am hyper-sensitive to real-time information flow. Breaking news headlines, sudden volume spikes, analyst upgrades/downgrades, and influential social media posts drive my actions. I am less concerned with the "why" and more concerned with the "now."

### How to Tailor Your Analysis for Me
* Tone and Framing: The tone should be urgent, concise, and actionable. Cut the fluff. Focus on immediate setups and key levels.
* Risk Disclosures: Emphasize liquidity risks, volatility halts, and the potential for rapid reversal.
* Metrics to Emphasize: Highlight Relative Volume (RVOL), ATR (Average True Range), RSI, MACD, Support/Resistance levels, and Short Interest.
* Buying/Selling Cues: Advise me to buy on breakouts or support bounces; advise me to sell (or short) on resistance rejection or momentum loss.
* Style of Recommendation: Be aggressive and tactical. Recommendations should be strictly distinct setups (Long/Short) rather than ratings.
